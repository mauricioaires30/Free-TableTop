# Free TableTop

**English** · [Português (Brasil)](README.pt-BR.md)

A free virtual tabletop for RPGs that runs in the browser. Campaigns are saved in a folder on the game master's PC. No account, no uploads, no tracking.

**Open it:** [mauricioaires30.github.io/Free-TableTop](https://mauricioaires30.github.io/Free-TableTop/)

## Features
- **Campaign in your own folder:** the GM picks a folder once and everything is saved there: board, images, sheets, chat, journal and wiki. Works in Chrome and Edge. Other browsers keep the campaign in the browser itself, with a `.zip` backup to download and import.
- **Private tables:** every table has a random ID and an invite link (or QR code). Nobody gets in without the link, and tables never see each other.
- **Free board:** pan and zoom, notes, clue cards with a secret back side, and images you can drag in, paste or upload. Every item has an eye button, seen only by the GM, to show or hide it from the players (notes and clues start hidden). Players only move their own token and can't click what the fog hides.
- **Undo:** Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y (or Ctrl+Shift+Z) on the board. The GM undoes their own changes; each player undoes their token's moves.
- **Live cursors:** everyone sees where the players are pointing. The GM's cursor stays hidden.
- **Permissions on everything:** choose who can **see**, **move** and **edit** each item, sheet or page: everyone, only the GM, the owner, or specific players. Hidden things never reach the players' browsers.
- **Sheets for any system:** when creating the table, the GM picks a system (generic, d20 fantasy, paranormal investigation, dice pool, percentile roll-under, narrative Fate or blank) and gets that sheet plus a creature sheet. The GM edits the template (attributes, resource bars, text, lists, rolls, how the modifier works). Players don't pick templates: they just type a name and get the sheet the GM chose. Each player sees only their own sheet, and the GM can share any sheet. Templates can be exported and imported.
- **Campaign attributes:** the GM defines which attributes exist in their system (Strength, Agility, Luck...). A new attribute shows up on every sheet right away.
- **Inventory, equipment and powers:** lists on the sheet with quantity, equipped, notes and per-item rolls (e.g. weapon damage), for characters, NPCs and monsters.
- **Portraits:** the GM sets the image of every character, NPC and monster, shown on the sheet and the token.
- **Who changes what:** players handle their own sheets and health; the GM handles NPCs and everything else, and can unlock a player's sheet to fix something.
- **Rolls from the sheet:** click an attribute to roll its formula, like `1d20+{mod}` or `{v}d20kh1`.
- **NPCs and enemies:** hidden sheets only the GM sees, with secret initiative. Characters and NPCs have one token each; an enemy sheet (like "Goblin") becomes as many numbered tokens as the GM wants, each with its own health. Only the GM puts tokens on the map and links them.
- **Health on the tokens:** everyone sees the players' health above their tokens (NPCs and enemies too, if the GM allows). The bar, name and conditions always stay level, even when the token is rotated.
- **Combat:** initiative order (highest or lowest first), rounds and turns, hidden creatures, enemy tokens joining with one click, damage or healing right on the health bar, and token conditions the GM defines.
- **Map:** square or hex grid with snapping, layers (map, objects, tokens), locked backgrounds, tokens linked to sheets with health and conditions, fog of war (rectangle and brush), shared ruler with distances, and pings. Rotate the selected item with the mouse wheel. Stack several maps and switch between them with the eye.
- **Live images:** replace an image file in the campaign folder and the board updates for everyone.
- **Parchment look:** sheets, notes and the dice tray look like aged paper, with wax-seal modifiers and ruled inventory lines.
- **Dice tray:** a tray on the board to build rolls by clicking dice, with modifier, advantage, and its own roll history; each die spins and lands on its number. Includes d2, d3, d30 and custom dice. `2d6+3`, `4d6kh3`, `2d20kl1`, exploding `1d6!`, `d%`, success pools `5d10>=8`, roll-under `1d100<=65`, Fate dice `4dF` and rerolls `2d6r1`. Rolls can be public, private to the GM, or hidden.
- **Chat just for talking:** messages and `/w name` whispers; rolls stay in the dice tray.
- **GM tools:** session journal (with a draft from the day's chat), campaign wiki with `[[page]]` links, random tables with weights and nested tables, ready-made generators (names, taverns, weather, encounters, treasure, hooks) and progress clocks on the board.
- **Word filter:** catches offensive words in Portuguese, English and Spanish, including accents, `p0rr4`, `p.o.r.r.a`, repeated letters and endings like *-ing*. By default each person chooses on their own screen whether to hide them; the GM can also censor or block them for everyone and edit the list.
- **Moderation:** mute, kick, and generate a new invite.


## Disclaimer
The table only works while the GM has the campaign open. Each GM is responsible for the content shared at their table. The fog of war is visual: to truly hide something, also use permissions.
